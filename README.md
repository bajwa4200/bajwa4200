I am a versatile **Full-Stack Software Engineer** and **CEO** with 3+ years of experience building scalable web applications, mobile apps, AI systems, and distributed infrastructure — from ground-level architecture to shipped product.

Currently leading **EXO-Δ** (digital transformation agency), driving **B2B growth at TekScaleUp**, and steering **3DEVS** — while pursuing a **B.S. in Computer Software Engineering at SZABIST Islamabad** (Grade: A+).
